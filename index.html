Meta Lead Agent

In development

A JavaScript-based business research and lead collection tool using Crawlee and CheerioCrawler.

What it does

Crawls public business websites to extract titles, contact information, email addresses, phone links, Instagram and Facebook links, contact pages, and structures the collected data into JSON.

Responsible use

Respect website terms, access controls, rate limits, privacy requirements, and applicable laws. AI-based qualification, orchestration, and automated outreach are treated as separate stages unless verified.
